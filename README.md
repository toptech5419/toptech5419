## 🔬 Research & Engineering Depth

### 🤖 MSc Dissertation — Semantic Visual SLAM for Vineyards &nbsp;·&nbsp; _completed 2026_ ✅
**Integrating Keypoint Semantic Integration (KSI) into the open-source [pySLAM](https://github.com/luigifreda/pyslam) framework** for robust mapping in repetitive, perceptually-aliased vineyard scenes. MSc research at the **University of Lincoln — Lincoln Centre for Autonomous Systems (L-CAS)**, supervised by **Dr Riccardo Polvara**.

Standard visual-SLAM feature matching breaks down where scenes look alike — rows of near-identical vines — causing tracking loss and drift. KSI enriches keypoints with **semantic context** so matching stays reliable where geometry alone fails.

- Integrated **YOLOv9 semantic segmentation** with **SuperPoint** features as a plug-in for pySLAM, and extended its **C++ optimiser** so semantic weights reach bundle adjustment
- Ran a controlled benchmark of **65+ experiments across four seasons**, verified against an independent implementation, identified and corrected a confound, and profiled each module — finding that **half the latency came from data-handling overhead**
- Showed through statistical testing that the method **improves recognition of near-identical places**, but not overall localisation accuracy — reported honestly, as measured

`Python` · `C++` · `PyTorch` · `OpenCV` · `YOLOv9` · `SuperPoint / LightGlue` · `pySLAM` · `evo`

**🔗 [Read the write-up](https://lnkd.in/p/eVKruMxa)**
